I am a Physicist and Mathematician. My research philosophy emphasizes that meaningful research must be grounded in solid theoretical foundations.


For me, one of the most enjoyable aspects of research is translating the behavior of natural phenomena into mathematical language in order to construct models. I strongly believe that a solid understanding of theory helps us better recognize both the strengths and limitations of our models.


At the same time, models are only approximations of reality. While Mathematics can be derived through a sequence of logical steps — what I would describe as the “purest form of sincerity”: if A, then B — applied research also demands sincerity in other forms: ensuring the quality of our data, verifying that the assumptions of our models are satisfied, and reporting not only our findings, but also the degree of confidence we place in them after rigorous validation.

My research interests are interdisciplinary. My studies in several areas of Physics and Mathematics led to a deep interest in Statistics, Statistical Mechanics, Dynamical Systems, Stochastic Processes, and Probability Theory.


More recently, I have also been exploring Machine Learning topics such as Training Dynamics and Optimization. While I remain open to continuously expanding my interests, I am currently focused on developing deeper understanding and integrating that knowledge into applied modeling problems in areas such as Finance, Time Series Analysis, Physical Systems, and Sports Analysis.
