<section class="abstract">
  <h2>Thesis</h2>
  <p>Denoising gives a concrete way to learn local information about the data distribution. This makes it a useful bridge between classical score matching and modern generative modeling.</p>
</section>

<section class="claim-list" aria-label="Claim summary">
  <article>
    <b>Claim 1</b>
    <p>The corruption process defines the scale at which the score is learned.</p>
  </article>
  <article>
    <b>Claim 2</b>
    <p>The denoising target is easier to simulate than the normalized density.</p>
  </article>
</section>

## Takeaway

The central idea is local: instead of learning the full density, learn the direction that moves corrupted samples back toward the data manifold.
