    <h2>Method</h2>

    The estimator is trained on samples from a symmetric stable law and evaluated against a smoothed finite-difference approximation of the log-density gradient.

    <div class="equation">
    $$
    \widehat{s}_{\theta}(x) \approx \nabla_x \log p_{\alpha}(x)
    $$
    <small>Target score</small>
    </div>

    <h2>Code</h2>

    <div class="code-window">

sigma = 0.8
seed = 42

samples = stable_sample(alpha, sigma, n=50000, seed=seed)
