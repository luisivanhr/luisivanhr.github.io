    <h2>Discrete Couplings</h2>

    If \(a\) and \(b\) are discrete probability vectors, a coupling is a nonnegative matrix \(P\) such that

    <div class="equation">
    $$
    P\mathbf{1} = a,
    \quad
    P^\top\mathbf{1} = b.
    $$
    <small>Coupling constraints</small>
